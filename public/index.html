Why Nosara

Most leaders don’t burn out in a day. They wear down quietly, one postponed night of sleep at a time. We built Nosara to reverse that, with medicine, psychology and the Pacific.

The method

Three movements, one reset.

  1. Days 1–2

    Arrive

    A full medical and psychological baseline. We slow the body down before we ask anything of the mind.

  2. Days 3–5

    Reset

    Sleep, movement, breath and one-to-one work with our physician and psychologist. Evidence-based, never extreme.

  3. Day 6 onward

    Return

    A plan built for your real calendar, and ninety days of people checking in once you are home.
